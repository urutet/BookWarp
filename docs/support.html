Common questions

Which book formats can I import?

BookWarp supports EPUB, PDF, and FB2. Open the Library tab, tap the add button, and choose a file from Files.

Why does translation need a download?

BookWarp uses Apple’s on-device Translation framework. iOS may need to download a language model the first time you use a language pair. After that, translation runs on the device.

How do I practice words I saved?

Open the Learn tab to review saved vocabulary with quizzes, cards, and context. Saved sentences appear there too.

Where are my books and progress stored?

They are stored in BookWarp’s app storage on your device. Your device backup settings may also include that data in iCloud or computer backups.

How can I remove my data?

Use Settings → Delete All Data to remove your local BookWarp data. Manage any iCloud or computer backups separately in your device settings.
