Privacy
policy.

BookWarp is designed around your own books and learning. Here is how the app and this website handle data.

Last updated
25 September 2026

What BookWarp stores

Your imported books, reading progress, bookmarks, saved words and sentences, and app preferences are stored in BookWarp’s app storage on your device. BookWarp does not operate a server that receives this data. iOS may include app data in iCloud or computer backups according to your device’s backup settings.

Translation

When you translate selected text, BookWarp uses Apple’s on-device Translation framework. iOS may download a language model from Apple for a language pair before translation is available. BookWarp does not send the text you are reading to a BookWarp server.

Files you import

When you add an EPUB, PDF, or FB2 file, BookWarp copies it into its own app storage so you can keep reading if the original file moves. The app’s copy stays on your device unless it is included in a backup managed by iOS.

Accounts, analytics, and advertising

BookWarp does not require an account or sign-in. The app does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

This website

This static website does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or contact forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process standard connection information when you visit it. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details about GitHub’s services.

Your choices

You can delete books and saved items in the app. Settings includes a Delete All Data action for removing BookWarp’s local data. You can also remove the app from your device. Device backups are managed separately in iOS or on your computer.

Changes and questions

If BookWarp’s data practices change, this page will be updated. For questions, visit the support page.
